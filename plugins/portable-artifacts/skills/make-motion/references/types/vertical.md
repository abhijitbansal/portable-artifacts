# Type of film: vertical

A short for Reels, TikTok and Shorts: `"mode": "viral"`, 9:16, usually 10 to 20 seconds.

## When

The film is meant for a feed, often watched with the sound off, by someone who has not asked for it. If the case needs a minute to build, use the explainer page.

## Shape

- First line: who the short is for, in that viewer's own words. Open on the hook, not a title; the first shot reads from frame 0 and is a few words.
- The next one or two lines say what that viewer wants, or what hurts.
- The first useful point lands inside the first 10 seconds.
- Where the content has steps, promise a counted result early ("three steps") and deliver that count.
- Once, about midway, one line opens a second question, and the next line answers it.
- End on one ask and stop. A soft ask, in the `end` shot.
- Pace is 175 words a minute. Change what is on screen at least every 3 seconds; the mode flags a picture that sits still longer.
- This is craft, not proof: no view count or retention figure backs it. The engine has no continuous take and no endless zoom, so the motion is cuts and landings.

## Devices

- A `statement` that reads from frame 0, with `captions` for the viewer who has no sound.
- A `punch` or `slide` cut, to change the picture at least every 3 seconds.
- A `number` that lands on the word that says it.
- A `compare` for before and after.
- Cuts on the beat, with `music.snap`, when the short has a music bed.

## Check

- What the hook names is on screen in the first shot.
- A promise the film does not pay off is cut from the hook, and no number, result or deadline appears that the brief did not give.
- A "wrong way, then right way" film shows the right way.
- At most two hook devices in one opening.
- Captions are recommended (`"captions": true`), and no picture sits still for more than 3 seconds.
