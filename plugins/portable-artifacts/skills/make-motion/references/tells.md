# What gives each look away

A tell is a choice that shows a look was used without thought. Each tell says when the same choice is right. Every look is open to every film: these are the ways of using one badly. Under each look is one line on the content it suits.

## desk
- A numbered kicker slug on every shot, until the numbers stop meaning anything; works when the film is a numbered walk-through and the steps are the point.
- The highlighter on every starred word; works when the film marks only the two or three phrases it wants remembered.

Suits an editorial walk-through, or a story told in order.

## pop
- A caption stack of six or more words a line, so the capitals shrink and read as a paragraph; works when the line is a quote the short wants read slowly.
- `punch` or `slide` on every cut; works when the cuts are a fast run of items and each one is a new thing.

Suits a fast short.

## cosmos
- The glowing accent on several things at once, so nothing glows; works when the film is about light or signal and the glows are the subject.
- The `ripple` cut on every cut; works when the film moves between scales and each ripple marks a jump.

Suits a science explainer.

## ledger
- A chart of eight or more marks with no highlight, so the grid is all the eye finds; works when the claim is the pattern as a whole and the marks stay readable.
- The `highlight` red on a mark the line never names, so it points at nothing the viewer hears; works when a later line in the same shot names that mark.

Suits data.

## poster
- Every shot a `statement` in the heavy capitals, so the film is a row of type and nothing is drawn; works when the film is a spoken manifesto of short lines.
- A sentence of ten or more words in the heavy capitals, which shrinks the type until it stops being loud; works when the line is a quote the film holds on screen to be read.

Suits a loud claim.

## keynote
- Product screens on cards when there is no product to show; works when the card holds the one screen the film is about.
- A `blur` or `cross-zoom` cut on every cut; works when each cut is a reveal of the next part of the product.

Suits a product launch.

## marker
- More than one idea on a board; works when the second is a label for the first.
- The `erase` cut between every pair of shots, which costs 1.8 seconds each time; works when each board ends a section and the erase is the break.

Suits a whiteboard talk.
