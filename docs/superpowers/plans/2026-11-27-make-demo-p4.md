# make-demo phase 4 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The walkthrough end to end. One request goes from a URL to a click plan, a take, a draft film written by rule,
an MP4 and a guide page. `walk` ships in keynote and desk with `whole` stops and lifts only.

**Architecture:** make-demo gains four optional fields in `demo.json`: `look`, `opening`, `ending` and `brand`. Scout proposes
a brand (name and accent, with where each came from), and the click plan shows it for approval. A new `film.mjs` joins
`demo.json`, `scout.json` and one take folder offline. It recomputes the plan hash, refuses a take of another plan, and writes a
make-motion film by fixed rules, with no model. A new `guide.mjs` writes a make-explainer spec from the same inputs and the
rendered MP4. In make-motion, a `step:` stop is refused unless the look's `kinds.walk.pushes` is true (no look sets it). The
gate films become whole-and-lift films, and the look gate runs again.

**Tech Stack:** Node 20+, Playwright 1.64.0 (already pinned), ajv (already in make-demo), make-motion v2, make-explainer. No
new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-make-demo-design.md` sections 6.1, 6.7, 6.8, 6.11, 8 and 10; hand-off
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-27-make-demo-handoff-p4.md`; phase 3 plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-26-make-demo-p3.md` and
gate record `2026-11-26-make-demo-p3-gate.md`.

## The claim of this phase

1. `film.mjs` writes a film that make-motion validates, from an approved plan's take, with no model call. It writes one walk
   shot per use case, with one sentence per finished step. No page text can change the sentence count or add make-motion
   markup.
2. `film.mjs` and `guide.mjs` refuse a take of another plan: a `demo.json` whose bound fields (brand included) changed after
   the take, or a take whose steps are not the plan's. They never overwrite a file, and they never write outside the demo
   folder.
3. A brand reaches the film only when the click plan showed it and the take was of that plan. A `third-party` demo carries no
   brand and gets a source credit.
4. `walk` ships in a look only after that look's gate passes with whole-and-lift films. A `step:` stop is refused in every
   look until a later phase sets `pushes`.
5. The model never opens a signed-in take's frames, or a sheet, film or guide made from them. Nor does any reviewer agent.
   `film.mjs` and `guide.mjs` print that notice for a signed-in take.

A finding outside this claim that is not an obvious failure goes to "Known limits".

### Task 5: make-demo: `film.mjs`, the draft film by rule

**Files:** create `scripts/film.mjs`, `scripts/lib/film-draft.mjs`, `scripts/lib/say-line.mjs`, `scripts/lib/take-read.mjs`,
`scripts/lib/demo-out.mjs`; tests `test/say-line.test.mjs`, `test/film-draft.test.mjs`, `test/take-read.test.mjs`,
`test/film-command.test.mjs`. Extend `test/helpers/fixture-take.mjs` so it can also write the `demo.json`, `scout/scout.json`
and `click-plan.json` it used, giving the command test a real demo folder.

**Interfaces:**
- `sayLine(row): string` gives one sentence, from the row's `do`, its target name and its value:
  - `goto`: `Open <path>` (the address path, `/` as "the start page").
  - `click`: `Click <name>`.
  - `hover`: `Point at <name>`.
  - `type`: `Type <value> into <name>`.
  - `select`: `Choose <value> in <name>`.
  - `press`: `Press <key>`.
  - `scroll`: `Scroll to <name>`, or `Scroll down`.
  - `wait`: `Wait a moment`.
  - `show`: `Here is <name>`.
  - `intent` rows: the intent words.
  Every piece goes through `sayable(text)`, which keeps `\p{L}\p{N}`, space, `,`, `'` and `-`, and turns anything else into
  a space. It collapses spaces and cuts at 60 characters on a word. Empty gives `this step`. The result is capitalised and ends
  with one `.`. So no `.?!`, `*`, `(`, `)` or `[` from the page survives inside a sentence.
- `readDemoTake(demoDir, name): { dir, signedIn, planKey, stoppedAt, steps: [{ id, do, hasBox }] }`. `name` matches `^take-[0-9]{1,4}$`.
  The realpath must sit inside `realpath(demoDir)`. `take.json` must be a regular file of at most 8 MiB by `stat`, checked
  before it is read. It must parse, with `version` 1 and `steps` an array of 1 to 200 entries, each with an `id` matching
  `ID_PATTERN` and a `do` among `VERBS`. `signedIn` is a boolean and `planKey` a string. `stopped` is null or has a `row`
  matching `ID_PATTERN` (returned as `stoppedAt`; the reason text is never read). Nothing else is read; make-motion's
  `readTake` checks the frames at render. Each refusal is `{ path: 'take.<field>', message }`.
- `outPath(demoDir, wanted, fallback): string` resolves `wanted` (or `fallback`). It refuses a path whose parent's realpath
  is outside `realpath(demoDir)`, a path that exists (any type, link included: "<file> exists; pass --out with a new name"),
  and an extension other than `.json`.
- `draftFilm({ spec, rows, scout, take }): { film, notes }`. It is pure and follows these rules:
  - Root: `version` 2, `mode` `pro`, `look` (`spec.look` or `keynote`), `format` `16:9`. `accent` comes from
    `spec.brand.accent`. No voice, music or sound.
  - With `opening`:
    - A `statement` shot `hook` whose `say` is the hook.
    - Then, per fact, `fact-<k>`. A fact with `value` is a `number` with `value`, `label` (the fact's text cut to 40) and
      `say` (the text). A fact without one is a `statement` whose `say` is the text.
  - Per use case with at least one finished take step, a `walk` shot `uc-<use case id>` with:
    - `src` (the take folder's name), `frame` `window`, `title` (the use case title).
    - `steps`: the take's finished steps of that use case, in take order.
    - `say`: their `sayLine`s joined by a space.
    - `lift`: `[{ step }]` for the last of those steps with a box, else none. No `stops`.
  - The first walk shot gets `cut`: `zoom` in keynote, `crossfade` in desk.
  - With `ending`, an `end` shot `close`:
    - `say` is `ending.say`, else `That is <brand name or the URL's host>.`
    - `ask` is `ending.ask`.
    - `mark` is `brand.name` for `own` or `permitted`. For `third-party` it is `Source: <host>`, there is no accent, and the
      ask is kept.
  - `notes` lists a use case left out (no finished step), a take that stopped (named by `stoppedAt` only), and a missing
    ending.
- Checks before drafting, each refused with its path:
  - `planHash(spec, rows)` must equal the take's `planKey` minus `demo-`: "this take is of another plan: demo.json changed
    after the take (a step, origins, mask, session or brand). Undo the change, or plan, approve and take again".
  - Every take step id must be a row id.
  - A fact whose `from` is an address must be one of `scout.pages[].url`.
- `node <skill-dir>/scripts/film.mjs <demo.json> --take take-<n> [--out <file>] [--json]` writes `<demo>/film.json` by
  default. It prints the path, the shot count, the steps per walk shot and every note. It then prints the next command,
  `node <make-motion>/scripts/make.mjs <film> --json`, with the sibling path resolved. A signed-in take adds `notice: this
  film plays a signed-in take: its sheets and MP4 are the user's to look at; do not open them or send them to an agent.`
  Exit codes: 0 written, 1 refused (spec, take or plan errors), 2 usage.

## How it is built

Six builder dispatches (Tasks 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7), in order, Sonnet at high effort. The main session reviews each diff, reruns
the tests, commits and pushes. Task 4 is run by the main session: up to two Opus look reviewers per round (two rounds at
most), and one Sonnet revision dispatch per failed look per round. Then one Opus reviewer (Task 8). No other agents.

## Known limits, to be stated

- The draft film is silent, 16:9 and walkthrough only. Its sentences are plain placeholders built from control names, for
  the agent to rewrite.
- Whole view only. Page text shows at about 1.17 times its CSS size at 1080p on a 1280-wide take (13 px shows as about
  15 px), and a lift adds 1.1 times. Small text stays small; no stop pushes in.
- The hand-off cut opens from the centre, not from a named thing.
- Brand: a name and an accent only, from `og:site_name`, the title and `theme-color`. No logo, no colour read from the page's
  own styles. A site without `theme-color` gives no accent.
- `film.mjs` reads only `take.json`'s ids and flags. Frames are checked when make-motion renders.
- The guide inlines the 3x stills: a long guide weighs several MB.
- No storyboard for yes 2. The user watches the MP4.
- `walk` in `cosmos`, `ledger`, `poster` and `pop`: not yet.
